# E418 — Radial sensitivity and first OFF arrival (pre-registered on 2026-11-03)

> Français : [README.fr.md](README.fr.md)

**Synthetic scenario (never presented as real zeros).** At the real height T = γ_r (ranks 1, 100, 2000), the line member
½ + iT is replaced by (1) a single Tower β + iT and its conjugate (control, breaks the symmetry s ↔ 1−s);
(2) the mirror pair β + iT, 1−β + iT and their conjugates (complete quartet, symmetries of ζ respected), β ∈ {0.3, 0.45, 0.49}.
All other heights are kept.

**Measures.** displacement of the contacts (arctan(T/β) − arctan(2T))/T, amplitude ratio x^{β−½}, moduli 1/|ρ|; variation
of the contributions and of the accumulations (ΔΛ̂ᴮ, ΔΛ̂ᴬ on W1); reclassifications and reading errors of the breaks (rule E416);
complement needed to preserve the reading (= −Δ); **provable bound** on the defect: by finite increments on the
second difference of x^{s+1}/(s(s+1)) (second derivative x^{s−1}), |ΔΛ̂ᴮ(n)| ≤ 2[(n−1)^{−½} + Σ_{ρ new} (n−1)^{Re ρ − 1}];
collective response: least squares on the amplitudes of 1000 line zeros (heights kept) to cancel Δ,
and observation that the coefficients obtained are not integer multiplicities.

**Results.** `RESULTS.md`, `data/e418_results.json`, `logs/e418.log`.
