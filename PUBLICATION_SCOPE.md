# Publication scope

> Français : [PUBLICATION_SCOPE.fr.md](PUBLICATION_SCOPE.fr.md)

Public export dated 15 November 2026, with a fresh history, of the private research repository `zeta24-lean`. This document describes the **selection**: it is a publication scope, not a concealment. The experiments E400 to E477, needed to assess the claims of `README.md` and of the master documents, are included. The earlier RHV2 to RHV5 campaigns and the experiments prior to E400 remain in the private research repository and can be provided on request to the author. Some historical documents and experiment notes remain in French.

Only files tracked by git in the private repository were copied (`git ls-files`), without links, preserving the files. No history is carried over.

- `formalization/RHV6Fresh/`: library `RHV6Fresh/` (including `Zeta23Port` and `PNTPort` with their `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, `PROVENANCE.md` intact), `RHV6Fresh.lean`, `RHV6FreshCertificates.lean`, `lakefile.toml`, `lean-toolchain`, `scripts/`, `audits_compilation/`, the three master documents `RHV6final_STRATEGIE_*.md` (and the older `RHV6_STRATEGIE_*.md`), `HANDOVER_20261107_LIGNE.md`, the `AUDIT_*.md` files and other documents of this folder, `README.md`.

- `formalization/rhv3_lean/`: everything (except `.lake/`).
- Root: `README.md` (adapted), `COPYRIGHT.md`, `LICENSE`, `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CITATION.cff`, `SECURITY.md`, `PUBLICATION_SCOPE.md`, `PUBLICATION_EXCLUSIONS.tsv`, `.gitignore`, `docs/ANNONCE_PUBLICATION_20261115.md`.
